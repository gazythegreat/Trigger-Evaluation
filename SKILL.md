# trigger-evaluation — evaluating whether an AI crash trigger has fired

Set by Raymond on 23 Nov 2026. This skill states ONLY the framework Raymond
himself set (31 Oct 2026, with the sub-indicators he directed) and a neutral
evaluation procedure. It deliberately contains NO current status, NO past
classifications, NO baseline figures and NO other agent's conclusions, so that
every agent who imports it approaches the question afresh and derives its own
verdicts from evidence it gathers itself.

## Purpose

Distinguish a valuation correction in AI names from a system-wide financial
crash in the AI build-out. One trigger firing is warning / correction-level
stress. Two or more firing together is an escalation. Monitoring only — this
framework produces assessments, never investment advice.

## The four triggers (Raymond's definitions)

1. **Financing flip** — Hyperscaler (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, plus
   Oracle) capital expenditure overtakes operating cash flow AND the build-out
   shifts to large-scale debt funding, rather than being funded mainly from
   operating cash flow.
   - *Neocloud sub-indicator:* neoclouds (GPU-only cloud providers —
     CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda, Crusoe, IREN, Nscale and similar) are the
     most leveraged, purest-play part of the build-out, so stress should show
     there first: total debt and debt growth, interest as a share of revenue,
     bond yields / CDS spreads, refinancing terms.
   - *Equity-pressure warning layer:* a WARNING layer under this trigger —
     not the trigger firing. Sustained multiple compression in AI names over
     weeks (not a single session); buybacks / capital returns cut because
     capex consumes operating cash flow; dilutive equity funding returning
     at scale or on adverse terms. Report warning-layer movements as
     warnings, never as firings.
2. **Utilisation fall** — GPU / data-centre utilisation falls materially,
   data-centre vacancy rises, or GPU rental prices fall materially: capacity
   stops being supply-constrained and starts sitting idle.
   - *Neocloud sub-indicator:* sold-out / allocation status ending,
     on-demand availability rising, discounting to fill clusters.
   - *Price-per-token sub-indicator:* track published per-token prices at
     inference providers and frontier API list prices, by tier. The
     direction alone is not the signal; the cause is. Price falls driven by
     discounting to fill idle capacity are this trigger's signature;
     efficiency-driven falls and premium tiers raising premiums amid volume
     growth are not.
3. **Revenue conversion failure** — Lab run-rate revenue (ARR) fails to
   convert into recognised, gross-margin-positive revenue for 2–3
   consecutive quarters, OR recognised revenue growth stalls while capex
   guidance keeps rising. (Definitional note: run-rate / ARR is an
   annualisation of a recent period; recognised revenue is what the
   accounts book. The two are never interchangeable in an evaluation.)
4. **Backlog cancellation** — Cloud/AI backlog or committed capacity is
   cancelled or renegotiated down. Slower backlog growth does not count;
   slippage or cancellation of unbuilt, uncommitted projects does not
   count.

## The evidence rule (Raymond's)

An opinion, post, video or article alone NEVER counts as a trigger firing,
however prominent its author. A firing requires corroborating financial or
market evidence of at least one of these classes: an earnings release, a
regulatory filing, a guidance change, a bond or CDS price move, published
utilisation / vacancy / rental-price data, or a backlog disclosure.
Price-only market moves are reported as price-only moves, with the absence
of corroboration stated.

## Expected cascade (Raymond's)

The triggers are a logical grouping, not a chronology; in a crash they are
expected to fire in a cascade spaced weeks to quarters apart:
- Demand-led: Trigger 2 → Trigger 1 → Trigger 3 → Trigger 4.
- Financing-led: Trigger 1 → Trigger 2 → Trigger 3 → Trigger 4.
The observed order is diagnostic of the origin. Progression from one stage
to the next is itself an escalation, even on different days. When a trigger
fires, state its cascade stage as well as its name.

## Evaluation procedure

1. **Gather afresh.** For each trigger, collect the current evidence from
   primary sources where they exist (filings, earnings releases, official
   company releases, published market-data series). Do NOT import any prior
   assessment, status, classification or conclusion from another agent's
   scoreboard, report or commentary — if you encounter one, treat it as a
   claim to be tested, not as a given.
2. **Test each trigger separately.** For each of the four: state the firing
   condition in the definition's own terms, the evidence found (with its
   class and date), and a verdict — **firing**, **not firing**, or **not
   determinable on the evidence available** (say what evidence is missing).
   Assess company by company where the definition names companies, and
   state the attribution.
3. **Classify price action honestly.** If the only movement is in prices,
   say so, and say which corroborating evidence classes were checked and
   found absent.
4. **Read the cascade.** If one or more triggers fire, state the stage(s)
   and the observed order, and whether it reads demand-led or
   financing-led.
5. **Report in this shape:**
   - One block per trigger: definition limb tested · evidence (class,
     source, date) · verdict.
   - A closing summary: how many triggers firing, cascade stage if any,
     and what single new datum would most change the assessment.
   - No re-reporting of a movement already assessed as if it were new:
     date every movement you rely on.

## What this skill deliberately does not contain

No trigger statuses, no company attributions, no figures or baselines, no
classifications of past episodes, no proposed amendments to the framework,
and no other agent's reasoning. Those are exactly what each evaluating
agent must produce for itself. If you believe a definition is wrong or
incomplete, say so separately and explicitly — do not silently evaluate
against a different definition.
